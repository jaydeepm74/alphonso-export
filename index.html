What exactly are we selling?

Fresh, GI-tagged Alphonso from Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg, Palghar, Thane and Raigad, picked mid-April to June, flown in gift boxes of 12.

World data stops at 6 digits, so “mango” imports also include guavas and mangosteens. Alphonso itself is visible only in India's national data.

Not pulp: pulp is a different business (sea freight, all year, factory buyers).

Average UK mango import price: $2.93 per kg (Trade Map 2025). India's average there: $2.86; Pakistan's: $4.61.

Alphonso's price over the UK average. Sell story, not volume.

Rivals (export similarity)

Mexico ≈ ⅔ of US mango imports, almost all year · Pakistan same season, same Gulf buyers, sells 1.8× India's value in the UK · Brazil, Peru lead UK volumes, mostly in India's off-season. Our defence: own the April–June window and the GI name.
